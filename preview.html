Meet Jessica

Kind, professional, and clearly loves dogs.

Reviewers reach for the same phrase about Jessica again and again: a genuine love for dogs. From her home parlour on Hall Gardens she grooms one dog at a time, kindly and without rushing, and owners say their dogs come home looking amazing: clean, soft and perfectly trimmed.

Frenchies, staffies, cockapoos and everything between have their regular slot here. The atmosphere stays calm, the welcome is warm, and dogs that arrived nervous leave pampered.

🐶 First Grooms

The first groom decides all the others.

A dog's first trip to the groomers shapes how they feel about every one after it. Jessica's reviews are full of first-timers: a frenchie who came out a different dog, puppies who felt comfortable from the moment they arrived.

First grooms here are short, gentle and positive, with time built in for sniffing about and settling down. It costs a little; it pays back for the rest of your dog's life.
